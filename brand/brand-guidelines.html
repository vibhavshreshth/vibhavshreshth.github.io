01 · Brand essence

Who we are.

A pre-sales consultant who started as a developer, founded a software company, and earned an MBA at IIM Bodh Gaya. The brand exists to show one thing: client needs get understood properly before anything is sold.

PositioningTurning client needs into clear software solutions
PromiseYou'll be understood before you're sold to
Audience

Hiring managers, sales and delivery leaders, founders and prospective clients.

Proof

44 months of experience · 80+ proposals · 50+ intro calls · 40+ BANT-qualified discovery calls.

Golden Circle

WHATHOWWHY
  • Why: every business is unique, so every solution starts from its real need.
  • How: Think → Understand → Execute (Golden Circle, POV, SMART goals).
  • What: discovery, business analysis, proposals, proof-of-concepts and demos.

Personality

Clear

Plain words, numbers, short sentences. Not jargon.

Curious

Learning in public; the site is a branding lab. Not know-it-all.

Practical

Shows the real process and tools. Not vague claims.

Playful

Glitch switch, red-eyed avatar, colour wave. Never hiding the content.

08 · Theme transition

200 ms of controlled chaos.

The brand's signature moment. When someone flips the light/dark switch, the site "crashes" into a cyberpunk version of itself for a split second, then lands in the new theme. Total length: 200 ms (T=200 in assets/js/theme.js).

The four phases

1 · Crash0–40 ms
2 · Cyberpunk40–164 ms
4 · Settle174–200 ms
PhaseTimeWhat happens
1 · Crash0–20%Heavy glitch on the current theme; a white flash from 11–25%
2 · Cyberpunk20–82%The whole site switches to the cyberpunk look, with a lighter glitch over it
3 · Re-crash73–87%Heavy glitch with a black flash; the new theme swaps in at 82%
4 · Settle87–100%The glitch fades out; the new theme is left calm

Glitch effects

Torn slices

4–10 horizontal strips inverted, hue-shifted and pushed sideways.

Colour bars

6–16 thin bars in magenta, cyan, yellow and white, cutting through the page.

Noise

Fine scanlines and static while the glitch is strong.

Shake

The page jumps up to 26 px sideways and skews up to 6°.

RGB split

Magenta and cyan ghost copies with extra contrast; about one frame in five fully inverts.

Red eyes

The avatar's eyes glow red, then fade out over 0.6 s.

Rules

  • Only on the theme switch. Never on page load, scrolling or any other click.
  • Keep it at 200 ms. Longer starts to feel like a real error.
  • Never a standing theme. Cyberpunk colours and fonts appear only inside the transition.
  • Reduced motion: keep the colours, flashes and cyberpunk frame, but no shake or skew.
  • Clicks during the transition are ignored, so it can't stack or loop.

09 · Imagery and icons

Hand-drawn, simple, consistent.

  • Illustration over photos: the line avatar is the face of the brand.
  • Diagrams such as the Golden Circle use the accent colour and rotate slowly.
  • Icons: simple line icons, 2 px stroke, rounded joins, in text or muted colour.
  • Social icons: solid circles in the text colour; accent on hover.

10 · Voice and tone

Like explaining to a smart friend.

Think → Understand → Execute.
Say
  • "80+ proposals using AI-assisted transcript analysis"
  • "Leave your name and a way to reach you"
  • Numbers and outcomes first
  • Internships labelled as internships
Don't say
  • "Leveraged cutting-edge AI synergies"
  • "Submit your details to proceed"
  • Adjectives first
  • Internships blurred into full-time roles

Rules

  • First person, active voice, short sentences.
  • Sentence case for buttons and labels; section titles end with a full stop.
  • Experience is stated as 44 months.
  • Feature names stay fixed: Work Timeline, Playground, Know More, Notify Me!
